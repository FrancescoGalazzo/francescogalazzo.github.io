I am an engineer-in-training with a strong interest in software, data, and intelligent systems. I enjoy understanding how things work from the ground up and then using that knowledge to build tools that are simple, robust, and useful in practice.

Recently, I have been learning how to design and deploy modern web experiences, including this site, which is based on the al-folio template and customized to reflect my work and interests. I like working close to real problems, where code, infrastructure, and user experience all matter.

This website is my place to collect projects, notes, and experiments as I keep learning.
